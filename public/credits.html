The flower models were modified for the designer: trimmed to the flower head, resized, and the petals recoloured.

The bows, stem and greenery were modified too: cut down to one frond, leaf or stem where needed, simplified, resized, and the bows recoloured. The paper textures are cut from the photographed paper in three of the models above, made greyscale so any paper colour can tint them, and, for tissue, given fold detail worked out from the creases.
